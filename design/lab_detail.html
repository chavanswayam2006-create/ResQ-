Aim & Theory Overview
In disaster scenarios, reports are uncertain and qualitative (e.g. "heavy smoke", "several casualties"). A Fuzzy Logic Controller maps real-valued inputs through triangular membership functions to fuzzy linguistic variables, evaluates rule firing via Min-operator inference, and defuzzifies via Centroid to obtain a continuous dispatch urgency.

Viva Quick Facts & Examiner Questions
  • Q1: Why use Fuzzy Logic over Boolean rules? Real-world disaster telemetry contains partial truths; Boolean thresholds cause sharp, brittle switching.
  • Q2: What is the defuzzification method used? Center of Gravity (Centroid) computes the center of mass of the combined output fuzzy set.
  • Q3: How many rules are evaluated? All 9 antecedent pairs ($3 \times 3$) fire concurrently in parallel.
